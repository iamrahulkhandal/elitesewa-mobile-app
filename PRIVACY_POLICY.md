# Privacy Policy for EliteSewa

**Last Updated:** November 8, 2024

## Introduction

EliteSewa ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

By using EliteSewa, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Mobile Number**: Required for account creation and authentication via OTP
- **Name**: Collected during profile setup
- **Email Address**: Optional, collected if provided during registration
- **Address**: Collected for service delivery and location-based matching
- **Profile Information**: Including profile photos, preferences, and settings

### Location Information
- **Precise Location**: We collect your device's precise location (GPS coordinates) to:
  - Find nearby service providers
  - Enable location-based service booking
  - Track service delivery
  - Provide location-based recommendations
- **Location is only collected when you grant permission and actively use location-based features**

### Payment Information
- **Payment Details**: Processed securely through Razorpay payment gateway
- **Transaction History**: We store transaction records for order history and support
- **Note**: We do not store your full credit card or payment card details. All payment processing is handled by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor.

### Usage Information
- **App Usage Data**: How you interact with the app, features used, and time spent
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Log Data**: IP address, access times, app crashes, and error reports

### User-Generated Content
- **Reviews and Ratings**: Reviews and ratings you submit for services
- **Photos**: Images you upload for service requests or profile
- **Messages**: Communications with service providers through the app

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

1. **Service Delivery**
   - Connect you with service providers
   - Process and manage service bookings
   - Facilitate communication between customers and service providers
   - Track service delivery and completion

2. **Payment Processing**
   - Process payments for services
   - Manage refunds and disputes
   - Maintain transaction records

3. **Location Services**
   - Find nearby service providers
   - Enable location-based service matching
   - Track service provider location during service delivery
   - Provide location-based recommendations

4. **Account Management**
   - Create and manage your account
   - Authenticate your identity
   - Provide customer support
   - Send important account-related notifications

5. **Improvement and Analytics**
   - Analyze app usage to improve our services
   - Develop new features
   - Fix bugs and technical issues
   - Conduct research and analytics

6. **Communication**
   - Send service-related notifications
   - Respond to your inquiries
   - Send important updates about our services
   - Marketing communications (with your consent)

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption**: Data transmitted over the internet is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
- **Secure Storage**: Personal data is stored securely on encrypted servers
- **Access Controls**: Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
- **Regular Security Audits**: We conduct regular security assessments

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
